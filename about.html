About Loona Target Tracker

Loona Target Tracker · Loona Automation

A daily report sheet that calculates itself

Loona Target Tracker is a free daily target-versus-achievement reporting tool for teams. It exists because thousands of field teams still end every working day by filling the same spreadsheet, fixing the same broken formulas, and photographing the result for a manager. This tracker keeps the familiar sheet your team already knows — your sections, your rows, your columns — and removes the fragile parts: you type only two numbers, target and done, and pending amounts and achievement percentages calculate themselves, correctly, every time.

Built around your team's real layout

Nothing here is fixed. Rename the sections to your hubs, portfolios or departments. Rename the rows to your centres, branches or team members. Add, rename and reorder columns with one tap. Columns come in four kinds: Number columns that add up, plain Text columns, Auto − columns that subtract one number column from another (Target minus Done gives Pending), and Auto % columns that divide one column by another (Done divided by Target gives Achievement %). Totals are always recalculated from the totals themselves — never averaged, never broken, never showing an error code in front of your manager.

Percentage cells change colour by thresholds you set yourself: green at or above your first target line, amber at or above the second, red below it, so a whole day's performance can be read at a glance. A worked example sheet is built in — tap Load Example to see a filled report, learn the layout in a minute, then start a New Blank Sheet, copy yesterday's targets in one tap, or remove sample data cleanly when you are ready for real figures.

Daily history, saved formats and one-tap sharing

Every day saves under its own date, so any past day can be reopened from the date picker and checked. Saved formats store your columns, section names and row names — without the numbers — so a weekly or regional layout returns in one tap. When the day is done, send the report as a PNG image in the familiar yellow-and-green spreadsheet style straight to any messaging app, print it, or export CSV, Excel and a full JSON backup that can be restored later.

Private by design, free by principle

Your figures never leave your device. The tracker runs entirely in your browser, stores everything locally, needs no sign-up and works offline as an installable PWA. There is no server copy of your team's numbers. Because the data lives on your device, use the Backup feature regularly — that small habit is the whole safety system.

The tracker is made for bank and microfinance branches, sales teams, school staff, delivery and service teams — any team, anywhere in the world, that reports targets daily. It is part of Loona Automation, founded by Aurang Zeb Loona, a school teacher and AI teacher trainer from Jaranwala, Faisalabad, Punjab, Pakistan, who builds free tools with no account walls and no payment doors, because the person who needs a tool should be able to simply open it and work.
