About Loona Target Tracker

Loona Target Tracker · Loona Automation

Loona Target Tracker is a free daily target-vs-achievement report tool for teams. You build the sheet the way your team already works — your sections (hubs, portfolios, departments), your rows (centres, branches, team members) and your own columns — then type target and done figures each day while pending amounts and percentages calculate themselves.

It is built for field teams everywhere: bank and microfinance branches, sales teams, school staff, delivery and service teams — anyone who reports targets at the end of the day. Your data is saved only on your own device.

Loona Target Tracker is part of Loona Automation, a brand by Aurang Zeb Loona that builds free, practical tools and AI services for everyday people and small businesses, for users everywhere in the world.

Why this tool is free

We believe useful tools should be within everyone's reach. Loona Target Tracker is free to use; the site may show advertising in the future to cover its costs, but the tool itself stays free.
