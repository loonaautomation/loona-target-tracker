Tracker questions we can help with

Formulas and columns: how Auto − columns subtract one number column from another, how Auto % columns calculate achievement, why totals are recalculated from totals rather than averaged, and how to set the green and amber percentage thresholds your team uses.

Layouts and formats: renaming sections, rows and columns, reordering columns, saving a format, loading the worked example sheet, copying yesterday's targets, starting a new day, and removing the built-in sample data from a device.

Sharing and files: sending the report as a PNG image, printing, CSV export and import, Excel export, and JSON backup and restore — including moving your sheet to a new phone.

Reporting a bug

Please include your device, your browser, and the exact steps: which date you opened, which column or button you used, what you expected and what appeared instead. If a total looks wrong, tell us the target and done figures for that row — example numbers are perfect; you never need to send your team's real figures. Screenshots help a great deal with layout problems.

Requests for new column types, new export formats and team workflows are welcome too — this tracker grows from the reports real teams send every day.
