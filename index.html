Columns — rename, reorder, add your own

Auto columns do the maths for you: an Auto − column subtracts one number column from another (for example Target − Done = Pending); an Auto % column divides one by another (for example Done ÷ Target = Achievement %). Totals are always recalculated from the totals — never averaged, never broken.

How it works

  1. Make it yours (one time). Rename the sections to your hubs or portfolios, the rows to your centres or team members, and add or rename columns until the sheet looks exactly like the report your team already uses — anywhere in the world.
  2. Type two numbers a day. Enter each centre's target and how much is done. Pending and achievement % fill in by themselves, row by row, with section totals and a grand total — always correct, never a broken formula.
  3. Send it in one tap. Export a PNG picture in the familiar yellow-and-green spreadsheet style and share it to any messaging app, or print it, or save CSV/Excel files.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Loona Target Tracker free?

Yes. Loona Target Tracker is completely free — no sign-up, no subscription and no limit on sections, rows or columns.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your own device, in your browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded to any server. Use the JSON backup to move your data to another phone or computer.

Can I set my own percentage formula?

Yes. Add an Auto % column and choose any two number columns — the tool calculates A ÷ B × 100 for every row and for the totals. You can also add an Auto Pending column that subtracts any column from any other.

Can I use my own hub, centre and column names?

Yes. Everything is yours to name: the report title, section (hub) names, row (service centre) names and every column heading. Add, rename, reorder or delete sections, rows and columns at any time, and save your layout as a named format to reuse.

How do I send the daily report to my head office?

Tap PNG Image or Share to send a picture of the report — in the same yellow-and-green style as a spreadsheet — straight to WhatsApp or any other app. You can also print it, or export CSV and Excel files.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first load the tracker works offline, and your saved reports stay on your device.
